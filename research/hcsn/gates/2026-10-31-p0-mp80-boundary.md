The existing original He–Xiong Eq12 FP64 candidate can incur meaningful relative error from cancellation in Laplace-of-Gaussian-CDF expressions even though the input parameters are within the declared finite exploratory HX domain **and** the previous necessary local equity-curvature sign screen passes.

For A/B, original local Eq11 boundary cashflow values are negative (`-30.6735430523` and `-0.0016617311914`) and clear the prior empirical `-1e-9*scale` sign screen. Thus that screen does not detect the inaccurate FP64 Eq12 root.

MP80 independently reconstructs coefficients and Gaussian CDF Laplace integrals from parameter floats, with an independent mpmath context and analytic exact-zero-rate limit. This is **the same Eq12 identity evaluated with substantially higher arithmetic precision**, not an independent boundary PDE/obstacle proof. MP60/MP100 agree for the frozen witnesses at the checked float-output tolerance.

A deterministic wide 2,500-parameter random search on a locally reconstructed Eq12 analytic subset compared 1,609 positive finite candidates and identified the above two >1e-7 cases. This was an exploratory bug discovery, not preregistered uniform coverage.

## Fail-closed change

`hx_uniform.equity_logmargin` now calls `require_consistent_boundary(p, root)` immediately after its existing Eq12 supplied-boundary equality check, **before** deciding whether to return stopping equity or evaluate the local/A7/Green continuation. The diagnostic refuses a root if the FP64-versus-MP80 relative discrepancy is >1e-10 (or if candidate input is malformed). This tolerance is a **selected research diagnostic, not a directed-rounding theorem**; it can reject some legitimate cases, which is preferable to silently accepting numerically suspect candidates in a research gate.

The existing `global_obstacle_certified=false` and `production_activation=false` remain unchanged. `closed_boundary` itself is not silently relabeled as certified or rewritten; users of the raw scalar method do **not** automatically inherit this wrapper check.

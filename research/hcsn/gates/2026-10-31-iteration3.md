# HCSN P0–P6 Gate Iteration 3 — 2026-10-31 (CPU research-only)

## Verdict

**P0–P6 remain OPEN. Zero production gates closed.** The fail-closed status is intentional: no real multi-modal pretrained encoder, production power/GPU benchmark, strong multi-seed long-memory win, or independently demonstrated extra HCSN utility.

Source basis: He & Xiong (2012), *Journal of Finance*, eqs (8), (11), (12), (A5), (A7). The regenerative QVI and the standalone Put-like neural gate are **new constructs**, not derived equity/default probability from original paper.

### P0 analytical and state-dependent reset subgates

- Original HX bounded 84-case sweep: 84 successful; Eq12 vs independent smooth-pasting root max relative error `2.3707201284432297e-12`; max smooth log derivative `1.0956349053596038e-09`; non-singular A7 vs independent Green max relative error `3.6772319161487575e-10`. Ten cases exactly zero `xi*k` are *not* covered by direct A7.
- `hx_uniform.py`: when direct A7 has a removable degeneracy at `xi*k≈0`, use the independent **numerical** Green evaluator; when log-margin <=1e-3 use local fourth-order PDE expansion. This is not an analytical closed-form proof across zero liquidity, nor a fast GPU operator. Method provenance is explicit, and inputs outside stated range reject.
- `contextual_qvi.py`: true **nonlocal** state-dependent obstacle `M U(v)=U(R(v))-c(v)` solved by monotone spatial finite differences plus sparse implicit policy iteration. Without state dependence, compares with independent analytic fixed-reset boundary. With state dependence, validates global *discrete-grid* QVI complementarity and refinement. No independent all-domain continuous proof, state-dependent time-varying QVI or GPU certification.
- Very small log margins cannot be faithfully reconstructed from BF16/FP32 ratios; the source `p0_extreme_result.json` documents separate log-margin benefits and precision failure. A log-margin native channel is required in production designs.

### P1 mechanism and null hypothesis

- New **C1 Hermite control** uses a precomputed table on log-margin [-12,3] with 1321 knots, and preserves option value + first derivative at every knot. It shares the same 161 trainable parameters, train examples, initial seed, optimization steps and heldout states with the analytic feature.
- Heldout max feature value difference `5.96e-8`; signed VJP mismatch `9.54e-7`. Three seeds (11,23,37), 90 updates, 420 ID and 420 OOD fixed heldout states per seed: analytic and table lookup had **identical exact regret on all six reported comparisons** (within output precision). Analytic mean ID regret `0.0049442`; OOD `0.0119336`. No special net utility of per-token closed-form execution demonstrated.
- This table null is *derived from the analytic shape* and is **not** a plain ordinary gate or FLOP-matched ordinary baseline. Prior independent paired audit still shows ordinary two-gate *better* ID and OOD. P1 remains OPEN.

### P3 / P4 / P6 outstanding

- No accessible real pretrained text, speech, vision and video checkpoints plus heldout datasets within available CPU-only/no-direct-download environment; cannot validate embedding, alignment, negative transfer, modality ablations or medium-scale training. Do **not** label toy randomly-initialized encoders or synthetic fusion as evidence of these gates.

## Strict gate policy

P0 full closure requires exact paper domain and alternate verified numerical formulation with global parameter restrictions, quantization, sign conditions, and full-precision GPU/CPU consistency. P1 requires preregistered strong-control benefit under matched FLOP/parameter/local signed slopes. P2 requires cross-seed, trained multi-reset long-horizon generalization, hard/soft event equivalence & stability. P3–P6 require real models/datasets, independent heldout tests, hardware energy and trained scaling. **None of those are satisfied by this research bundle.**

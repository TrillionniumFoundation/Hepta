Illustrative bounded parameter calibration lowered event-time MSE from `0.6229476975096219` to `1.1921233598691295e-05` and estimated rate `0.5295881391326897` vs target `.53`. Event count is not differentiable; do **not** generalize this to learned stochastic event-time BPTT.

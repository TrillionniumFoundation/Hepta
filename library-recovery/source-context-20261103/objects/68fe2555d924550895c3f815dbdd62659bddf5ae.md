## Mathematical scope

A scalar log margin `X=x0+mu*t+sigma*W` hits zero, then picks a binary reset mode conditionally on the actual first-hit time `s`. Reset margin `R_z(s)`, refractory `rho(s)`, and selection `pi_z(s)` are deterministic causal functions of event time. The conditional second-event law is a single integral of the *first-event density* and *single-event analytic CDF* `F`, and the discounted transform similarly integrates `G`. Only the interior of the bounded FP64 CPU model is asserted; no general QVI, original HX full-domain proof, or gradient through a sampled event count.

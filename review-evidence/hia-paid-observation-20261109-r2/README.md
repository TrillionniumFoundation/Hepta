# Paid observation mechanism pilot — final runner

**PASS_MECHANISM; incremental NDU acceptance remains HOLD.** This separate
pilot executes actual paid schema observations and local repair effects. No
trained or native NDU paid-information policy is invoked: `NOT_RUN`, zero
policy calls. Existing scalar and multistep histories remain unchanged.

All **30/30 cells** ran: ten paired heldout tasks × Fixed, exact VOI and
independently enumerated finite MPC. Public priors, task fixtures, action rights
and original budgets match in every pair. Eight actual shared calibration
READs precede the frozen fixed-policy fit. The final runner and protocol bytes
remained unchanged throughout the campaign; every cell stayed within its
original observed tariff and wall budgets.

Scores are registered synthetic reward minus charged action fees. They are
not success-rate confidence bounds, nanoseconds, money, real-task gains or NDU
value. The limited fixed threshold family is not the strongest possible learned
baseline; exact VOI and MPC match here. The heldout partition is frozen and
withheld from online policy inputs, but is not independently blinded from the
harness author. The unit tests execute the same deterministic partition.

Paid READ changed the subsequent repair relative to the prior no-read choice
in **13 delivered cells**. READ uses real `PRAGMA table_info` subprocesses;
repair uses actual SQLite transactions and separate post-terminal row checks.
All three invalid-schema controls retain the failed READ process, fee, stderr
and unchanged database, with no follow-on repair. The unaffordable-repair task
preserves STOP in every arm. No effect is retried, refunded or labelled rolled
back. Trusted same-host call/child-process boundaries are not a capability
sandbox or external attestation.

`RESULT.json` contains actual outer/nested timings, decisions, original process
stdout/stderr and exits, budget charges, evaluator values, input/final database
hashes and all boundaries. Outer intervals include their nested operations;
their subtotals must not be added again. Shared calibration and fitting costs
are recorded once. Shared-host wall times are not a controlled speed comparison
or complete billed cost.

`SOURCE.py` and `PROTOCOL.json` are the exact execution inputs. `databases/`
retains all 38 final synthetic SQLite fixtures. `tests.log` records seven
unittest cases, including exact VOI/MPC comparison over **630 public states**,
real differing schema observations and effects, failed READ charges/no repair,
wrong repair retention, budget STOP and timeout UNKNOWN without retry.

The [first run](../hia-paid-observation-20261109/README.md), before the final
wording and explicit whole-matrix budget gate, remains intact. No result was
rerun to obtain a better score or replace a failed measurement.

The next P5 gate still requires an actual trained/native NDU paid-information
policy, matched training and tuning, independent real tasks, strong VOI/MPC
controls and complete measured costs. This mechanism result does not close it.

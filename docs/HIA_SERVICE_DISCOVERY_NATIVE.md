## Actual delivery and resources

A per-port foreign activation is separate from a weak-registered whole-method activation, which spans all ports until FINALIZE. A confirmed Store.profile child or port ACK cannot retire the parent's later obligations. Resources are captured only from actual native/ResourceScope handoffs under current identity/generation and PID/thread rules.

Each effect and native handoff rechecks pending resources and sticky loss. Safe initialization refusal differs from entered/unconfirmed calls. Same-Frame reentry refuses outside cleanup. Healthy local destruction precedes release ACK; a real child loss swallowed by a finalizer can still block that ACK. This does not infer a provider's private DB, resurrect dead weak receivers, or hold fake temporary Stores based on a getter.

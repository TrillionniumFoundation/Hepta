## Independent bridge correction

The old bridge could grant DONE/ACK permission after an entry or other leaf
caught an invalid reentry that had sealed the session. The new bridge has a
permanent closed bit plus an epoch. It checks the original ticket after entry
binding, memmove, image return, both ledgers, integer representation and frame
construction. All external entry methods reject closed sessions. A normal
finish consumes its one permission and then closes permanently. A leaf's
original BaseException is rethrown unchanged; caught invalid reentry followed
by a normal leaf return fails closed.

PASS and its 229 original files are not modified or attributed to this bridge.

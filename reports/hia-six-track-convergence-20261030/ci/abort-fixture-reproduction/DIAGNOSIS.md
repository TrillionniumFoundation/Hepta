The unchanged production supervisor enforces its three-second deadline and kills/reaps an overdue child. This is a plausible mechanism consistent with the CI observation, not an attested root cause: the CI core_pattern and child frame were not retained. The repair removes that known source of environmental interference within this controlled fixture.
